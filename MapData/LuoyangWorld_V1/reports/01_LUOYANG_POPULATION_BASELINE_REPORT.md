# Luoyang population baseline

- Low Population Profile: national actual-person profile 500,000; Luoyang region 10,271 permanent Persons, 2,231 Households, 6,157 effective workers.
- Recommended Population Profile: national actual-person profile 1,000,000; Luoyang region 20,542 permanent Persons, 4,498 Households, 12,175 effective workers.
- High Population Profile: national actual-person profile 2,000,000; Luoyang region 41,084 permanent Persons, 8,997 Households, 24,389 effective workers.

The recommended profile reuses M24's one-million actual-person opening scale. Historical Henan Yin population is only a 2.0542% spatial weight; it never substitutes aggregate population or resources for permanent Persons.
